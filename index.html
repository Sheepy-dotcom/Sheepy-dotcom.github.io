Milo Treasure Tunnels

A digging game for children. Dig down with Milo the mole, bring back treasure, dinosaur bones and eggs to hatch, and fill a museum with what you find.

  • Twenty-four places to dig, across six chapters.
  • Eleven dinosaur skeletons to piece together.
  • Seventeen eggs, and the pets that come out of them.
  • No account, no chat, and nothing a child can buy on their own.
